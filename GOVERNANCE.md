# Governance

LabTicker is an open-source project; a GitHub organization does not establish a legal entity. The owner retains final scope, brand, legal, expenditure, production and PMF decisions. The preferred organization handle `labticker` remains unconfirmed until allocation.

Maintainers review small PRs with passing required checks. Changes to source contracts, authentication, consent, migrations and deployment require designated reviewers. Protect `main`, block force pushes/deletions, require reviews and passing checks, and restrict production through an approved GitHub Environment. CODEOWNERS enforcement depends on actual repository settings; no such remote protections are claimed yet.

Discuss roadmap changes in public issues without publishing private customer or contractual information. Moderators may remove abusive content and restrict repeat offenders, recording a fair explanation where safe. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed. Sponsors cannot purchase rankings, alert inclusion, warning severity or editorial decisions.

Advertising remains absent through MVP/beta. Metrics never automatically declare PMF. Activation requires a separate explicit owner PMF and monetization decision, followed by provider, privacy and consent review.
